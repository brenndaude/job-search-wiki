# Research dossier

- **Company:** fictional warehouse robotics, 300 people. Every fact in a real dossier carries a source
  URL and a date.
- **People:** the hiring manager and one recruiter, each with a public hook (a talk, a post).
- **Process:** recruiter call, take-home, presentation, final interview.
- **Pay:** a range with sources; the ask and the floor go in `strategy.md`.
- **Could not verify:** list it explicitly.
