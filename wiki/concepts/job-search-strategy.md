# Strategy

## Positioning
Lead with what is rare: production forecasting with a measured result (12% against baseline), plus
software engineering before data science. Never sell the profile as « data analyst ».

## Targets, in order
1. Product companies with forecasting at the core (energy, logistics). 2. Consultancies with an ML
practice, as a fallback.

## Pay
Record sources and dates for every figure. Set the ask and the floor before the first call.
